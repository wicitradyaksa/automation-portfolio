Privacy Policy

Job Search Automation · last updated 18 October 2026

Job Search Automation is a personal tool used only by its developer, Ida Bagus Wicitra Dyaksa, to manage the developer's own job applications. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it uses

With the account owner's permission, the app reads email messages in the developer's own Gmail account to recognise replies from employers (application confirmations, rejections and interview requests), and creates draft emails that the developer reviews and sends by hand. It never sends email on its own.

Where the data goes

Data is processed and stored only on the developer's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Short excerpts of job postings, never the contents of your email, may be sent to an AI model provider to score job matches.

Limited Use

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token and database removes all stored data.
