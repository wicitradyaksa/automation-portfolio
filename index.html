How I work with you

No big-bang rollout. We start with the process that costs you the most and prove the saving before moving on.

Time the process

We map how the work is done today and time each step, so we automate the step that actually costs you, not the one that's merely annoying.

Build with a safety net

Every automation checks its own work and hands anything uncertain to a person. Nothing fails silently.

Test before it touches real data

Each workflow runs through its real-world scenarios first. My portfolio's seven workflows pass 53 automated checks.

Hand over and measure

You get alerts, a plain-English runbook, and the before/after numbers, measured on your process.
